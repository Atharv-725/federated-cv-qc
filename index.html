DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH

Cryptographic Privacy Architecture

Four foundational pillars engineered to prevent membership inference, deep gradient leakage (DLG), and intellectual property theft in mission-critical manufacturing.

1. Local Data Isolation

Proprietary high-resolution camera feeds and defect images never leave factory LAN perimeters. Training loops execute completely on on-premises edge compute.

2. Gaussian DP Perturbation

Gradient updates are clipped to $L_2$-norm bound $C$ and injected with calibrated Gaussian noise ($\sigma = 0.8$), ensuring mathematically bounded $(\epsilon, \delta)$ differential privacy.

3. DLG Inversion Immunity

By injecting differential privacy noise before transmission, malicious reconstructive gradient inversion attacks (such as Deep Leakage from Gradients) recover only pure random noise.

4. Flower FedAvg Aggregation

The central server computes coordinate-wise federated parameter aggregation, allowing cross-factory knowledge transfer without storing or reconstructing individual data records.
